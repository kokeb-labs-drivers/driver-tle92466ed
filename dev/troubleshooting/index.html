Troubleshooting¶

Symptoms, causes and fixes, cheapest check first. Section numbers refer to the datasheet, Rev. 1.2.

Datasheet sections this page relies on

From Infineon IFX-ntz1491827137291 1.2:

  • Supplies, VIO_SEL and the power-on reset: 4.1.1 TDS_Power Supply, p. 14
  • Voltage monitoring and the VBAT thresholds: 4.1.2 TDS_Voltage monitoring; Equation 1, p. 14
  • Reset State, Config Mode, Mission Mode and Critical Fault: 4.3.1 TDS_IC Operation states; Figure 4, p. 19
  • Open load or short to ground, thresholds and warning: 4.9.2 TDS_Open Load/Short to Ground (OLSG); 4.9.3 TDS_OLSG Warning, p. 34
  • Register and OTP ECC, the self-test: 4.9.7 TDS_Register/OTP ECC; 4.9.8 TDS_Built in Self Test (BIST), p. 39
  • Average current, duty cycle and VBAT feedback: 4.10.2 TDS_Average feedback values; Equations 18 to 22, p. 41
  • The reply pipeline, frames and reply status: 5.2.1 TDS_Data flow; 5.2.3 SPI frame definition, p. 50
  • Identity, ICVID and CHIPID: 5.3.2.19 Version Register; 5.3.2.24 Unique Chip Identification Number Register 0, p. 75

All of them, with the driver API for each: Datasheet map.

GetBusDiagnostics() counts frames, retries, rejected replies and resyncs, and GetLastReply() holds the last 32-bit word read: log both while you look for a bus problem.

EnsureInitialized returns Bus¶

The port reported a failure: its EnsureInitialized(), a Transfer() or a GpioSet() returned false.

  1. Check that your port's EnsureInitialized() returns true on its own (log it).
  2. Check the bus and control pins in your port against the board.
  3. With a logic analyser, check that the first frames appear on the bus: an ICVID read (0x..C00200), then FB_STAT reads.

EnsureInitialized returns NoDevice¶

MISO read all zeros, all ones, or exactly the frame just sent. Nothing drives SO, or MOSI reaches MISO.

GetLastReply() Likely cause Fix
0x00000000 VIO missing (SO is powered from VIO), the chip held in reset, SO not connected Check VIO, RESN high after the start, SO continuity
0xFFFFFFFF SO floating with a pull-up, or the chip unpowered Check VDD and VIO, CSN reaching pin 5
The frame sent MOSI and MISO shorted, or SO not soldered with MOSI coupled in Check the board

EnsureInitialized returns Crc or ReplyMismatch¶

Frames arrive, but not intact or not in step. The chip shifts SO on the rising SCK edge and samples SI on the falling edge: SPI mode 1 (section 5.1.1). Mode 0 typically gives Crc; a shared bus that interleaves another transfer gives ReplyMismatch. Check CSN high time between frames (600 ns minimum, Table 18), lower the clock, and make sure no other code sends frames to this chip during a driver call.

EnsureInitialized returns Timeout¶

INIT_DONE in FB_STAT did not read 1 within Config::init_timeout_us (section 4.2.1): the clock or the logic is not ready. Check VDD (4.5 V to 5.5 V), RESN released, and with an external clock the CLK signal and Config::external_clock_khz.

EnsureInitialized returns DeviceIdMismatch or DeviceFault¶

DeviceIdMismatch: ICVID did not read manufacturer 0xC1 with version 0xFC (A11) or 0xFE (A13) (section 5.3.2.19): another part, or another design step this driver does not know. DeviceFault: OTP_VIRGIN is set, the chip's trim was never programmed (section 4.9.7: "this bit must always be 0"); GetStartupFaults() shows it. Replace the part.

EnsureInitialized returns WriteMismatch¶

GLOBAL_CONFIG did not read back what was written: the chip did not take a Config Mode write. Check that nothing else drives the chip and that MOSI reaches SI (reads alone work without it).

EnterMissionMode returns MissionBlocked¶

A fault of Figure 4 holds the chip in Config Mode. ReadStatus() says which kind, ReadFaults() which flag:

Flag Likely cause Fix
VioUndervoltage or VioOvervoltage Config::vio does not match the board (reset value 5 V) Set Config::vio to V3V3 on a 3.3 V MCU
VddUndervoltage VDD below 4.5 V Check the 5 V rail
VbatUndervoltage No load supply, or vbat_uv_code too high Check VBAT; the threshold is 162 mV per count
SpiWatchdog The SPI watchdog expired ReloadWatchdog() often enough; then ClearFaults()
ClockFault The clock watchdog saw f_SYS out of range The CLK pin and the dividers
CentralOtWarning, CentralOtError The die is hot Cooling, load current

Then ClearFaults() and EnterMissionMode() again. A flag that sets again at once means the cause is still there.

Calls return CriticalFault¶

Every reply is a critical fault frame: the chip is in the Critical Fault state (section 4.3.1), after an internal supply fault or a clock that runs too fast. GetLastCriticalFault() holds the flags (section 5.2.3.5): bits 7, 6 and 5 are 1 when the 1V5, 2V5 and the ADC bandgap are fine, bits 4 to 0 are clock faults (too slow, too fast, digital clock too slow or too fast, watchdog reference missing). A flags byte of 0x00 almost always means VDD is missing. The driver accepts the state only from two critical fault frames in a row (one alone can be noise), then drives EN low and is uninitialised. Only RESN (EnsureInitialized() with GpioSet()) or a power cycle leaves the state.

A channel does not switch on¶

EnableChannel(..., Verify::ReadBack) returns WriteMismatch, or the current stays at 0:

  • A latched fault keeps it off. After an OLSG, OL, SG, OC or OTE the chip keeps the channel disabled until the flag is cleared and EN_CH is set again (sections 4.9.2 to 4.9.6). An OFF-state diagnosis can also latch OL or SG on a channel that was disabled with a setpoint still set. ReadFaults(), fix the load, ClearChannelFaults(), enable again.
  • Not in Mission Mode, or the chip left it (a supply or watchdog fault): EnableChannel() returns MissionBlocked; ReadChannelControl() shows it.
  • Setpoint 0: in ON state the setpoint must not be 0 (section 4.9.1).
  • EN low: ReadPinStatus().value.enable.
  • A slave: drive the master of the pair.

The current regulates badly or stalls¶

  • PWM_REG_WARN or I_REG_WARN in ReadFaults(): the integrator hit its limit or its threshold went negative (sections 4.6.2.4, 4.6.2.5). Set icc.min_int_thresh above 1 (the preset uses 2) and keep icc.auto_limit above min_int_thresh + 3.
  • The PWM frequency drifts: set a PWM target (icc.period_mant) within 110 Hz to 4 kHz and a controller gain.
  • A setpoint step overshoots: leave Autolimit on (icc.auto_limit_disable = false).

ReadChannelFeedback returns NotReady¶

  • Every time: the measurement period is the dither period (section 4.4.2) and DITHER_CLK_DIV resets to 0, so with dither.mant = 0 the feedback never updates. Give the channel a dither reference clock (CurrentControlChannel() sets 1 ms), even with no dither amplitude.
  • Sometimes: no new period since the last read; read at most once per measurement period.
  • The channel must be enabled with a setpoint above 0 in Mission Mode, with EN high.

Calls return UnexpectedReset¶

POR_event or RES_event read 1 in an FB_STAT anchor after the start-up had cleared them: the chip reset since the driver started it, usually a brown-out on VDD or a RESN glitch. The driver is uninitialised again; EnsureInitialized() configures it. If it repeats, check the supply, its decoupling and RESN.

Reads return Crc now and then¶

Single corrupted replies point at signal integrity: lower the bus clock, shorten the wires, add a ground next to the clock line. The driver retries a read up to Config::max_retries times (GetBusDiagnostics().retries); a write is never retried, so its caller sees the error.
