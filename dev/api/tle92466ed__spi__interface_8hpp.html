Detailed Description

The SPI port of the TLE92466ED driver: the CRTP base, its pin enums, the SpiPort concept, the bus requirements, and the frame codec and pipeline of section 5.2.

The driver never calls an SDK. It calls hooks on a class you derive from tle92466ed::SpiInterface<Self>:

Hook Contract
bool EnsureInitialized() noexcept Bring the bus and the control outputs up on the first call, each output at its idle level; true at once afterwards
bool Transfer(const uint8_t* tx, uint8_t* rx, std::size_t len) noexcept One full-duplex transfer of len = 4 bytes, both buffers given, CSN low for the 32 bits and high after
void DelayUs(uint32_t us) noexcept Wait at least us microseconds

Every frame is 32 bits in one CSN low period (section 5.1.1): a transfer of any other length is ignored by the chip. The port moves raw bytes: it never retries, never interprets the frame and never configures the chip. What the chip needs from the bus is below as constexpr values (kSpiMode to kFrameBytes, Table 18); a port checks its settings against them with static_assert.

Control pins and their idle levels (handbook KB-HW-018): the port drives each output to its idle level before it enables the pin's output driver and before the first transfer. RESN idles in reset (GpioSignal::ACTIVE, low: every channel off until the driver releases it), EN idles low (GpioSignal::INACTIVE: every channel off), DRV0 and DRV1 idle low (INACTIVE). FAULTN is an open-drain input. A pin that is not wired to the MCU returns true from GpioSet() without doing anything (RESN pulled up, EN tied high, DRVx tied to GND).
