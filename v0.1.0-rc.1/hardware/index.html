The five GND pins and the tab are the signal ground; each channel's power stage has its own GNDPx, which connects to the same ground. Set Config::vio to the VIO level (the MCU's I/O supply). CLK is the external PLL clock, 1 MHz to 8 MHz; with the internal oscillator, tie it to GND. The pinout, the block diagram, the circuit and both tables are drawn from docs/hardware.yml.

Inside the chip¶

Section 1, Figure 1: the central logic takes the control pins and the SPI registers and runs six identical channels, each with its own channel logic, current control and dither, diagnosis, current sense, overcurrent and temperature protection, and a low-side DMOS with its shunt. The power supply block reports undervoltage and overvoltage to the central logic; the PLL makes the system clock f_SYS.

Control pins¶

The driver drives RESN, EN, DRV0 and DRV1 only through the optional GpioSet() hook of your port, with logical levels (GpioSignal::ACTIVE on RESN means "in reset", which your port writes as a low level), and reads FAULTN through the optional GpioRead() hook. Their idle levels, which your port drives before it enables each output:

Pin Idle level Why
RESN Low, ACTIVE (in reset) Every register at its reset value and every channel off until the driver starts the chip
EN Low, INACTIVE Every channel off; the driver raises it in EnterMissionMode() and lowers it in EnterConfigMode()
DRV0, DRV1 Low, INACTIVE A direct-drive channel stays off until the application drives the pin
CSN High The SPI driver's job; the chip ignores the bus while CSN is high

Without GpioSet(), tie RESN and EN high (through a resistor) and DRVx low: EnsureInitialized() then waits t_POR instead of pulsing RESN and writes every register, since the chip may keep what an earlier run wrote, and HardwareReset() and SetDrivePin() return Error::Unsupported.

Every frame carries a CRC-8 SAE J1850 (polynomial 0x1D, initial value 0xFF, final XOR 0xFF) in bits 31..24, computed over bits 7..0, 15..8 and 23..16 in that order (section 5.1.2). The chip answers each frame during the next one (section 5.2.1):

Power and supervision¶

Supply Range (Table 6) Monitored (section 4.1.2)
VDD 4.5 V to 5.5 V; SPI works from 3.5 V UV 3.7 V to 4.5 V, OV 5.5 V to 6.4 V
VIO 3.0 V to 5.5 V Against the 3.3 V or the 5 V thresholds that VIO_SEL selects
VBAT 6 V to 18 V (4 V to 38 V with deviations) Config::vbat_uv_code and vbat_ov_code, 162 mV per count

A VDD or VIO fault ends Mission Mode and clears every EN_CH; a VBAT fault is reported only. With VIO_SEL on the wrong level (the reset value is 5 V), a 3.3 V VIO reads as undervoltage and EnterMissionMode() returns MissionBlocked. VDD draws up to 40 mA; VBAT up to 1 mA in normal mode and 10 µA with VDD and VIO off (Table 9).

Application circuit¶

Section 6.1, Figure 26, with all six channels drawn: 100 µF and 100 nF on VBAT, 100 nF on VDD and on VIO at the pins, 4.7 nF to 10 nF from each LOAD and FWD pin to ground, a freewheeling diode from each FWDx to the load supply, and 10 kΩ from FAULTN to VIO. Each load connects between the load supply (behind a high-side safety switch, with 100 nF on it) and its LOADx pin. Figure 26 also ties RESN and EN to the reset and watchdog outputs of the system's power-supply chip; here the MCU drives them through GpioSet(), as the driver expects.

Design decisions and bench steps¶

The datasheet is silent on these; the driver's choice and the step that confirms it on a bench:

What Driver's choice Bench step
RESN low time kResetPulseUs = 1 ms Scope RESN and SO: after the pulse and t_RESN the first FB_STAT reads INIT_DONE = 1
CSN timing of the port The ESP32 port runs 2 MHz so half a clock covers t_CSN_LEAD Scope CSN and SCK: lead and lag at least 250 ns, CSN high at least 600 ns
The don't-care bits of a read frame Sent as 1100000B Read ICVID at 1 MHz: 0xC1FC or 0xC1FE with status 0

Status and limitations lists every step still to run.
